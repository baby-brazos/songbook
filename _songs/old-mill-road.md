I walked along the old mill road
When the morning fog was low,
With a fiddle and a heavy load
And a long, long way to go.

> Oh, the river runs and the river bends,
> And it never asks me why,
> I'll be singing with my oldest friends
> By the old mill, by and by.

I passed a farmer at his gate,
He was mending up his fence,
He said, "Son, you'd better not be late,"
And it made a lot of sense.

The sun came up on the water wheel,
And the swallows circled high,
And I knew the home I'd always feel
Was the old mill, by and by.
